Liquidity sweep

Price trades beyond a prior swing high or low, then reclaims the level. A sweep alone is not an entry signal.

Market structure shift

Look for meaningful displacement and a break of a relevant swing. Distinguish a close from a wick.

Order block

A price area associated with the last opposing candle before displacement. Context and invalidation matter.

Fair value gap

A three-candle imbalance area that may act as a reaction zone, not a guaranteed fill or reversal.
