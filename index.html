SETUP 01

Liquidity sweep

Price trades beyond a prior swing high or low, then reclaims the level. A sweep alone is not an entry signal.

  • Mark obvious external liquidity.
  • Wait for rejection or displacement.
  • Require lower-timeframe confirmation.
  • Define invalidation before entry.
SETUP 02

Market structure shift

A potential change in short-term order flow after a meaningful liquidity event.

  • Identify the relevant swing.
  • Look for decisive displacement.
  • Distinguish a close from a wick.
  • Do not force a bias in chop.
SETUP 03

Order block

A price area associated with the last opposing candle before displacement. Context matters more than the label.

  • Require clear displacement from the zone.
  • Prefer alignment with higher-timeframe context.
  • Plan stop beyond structural invalidation.
SETUP 04

Fair value gap

A three-candle imbalance area that may act as a reaction zone, not a guaranteed fill or reversal.

  • Check impulse quality and location.
  • Note premium / discount context.
  • Use confirmation and fixed risk.

Educational notes only. These concepts are discretionary and do not guarantee an outcome. Backtest rules with realistic spread, slippage and fees before using them live.
