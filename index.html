Models: SMC sequence (confirmed swing liquidity sweep → close-through MSS → three-candle FVG), EMA crossover and Donchian breakout. SMC swings are confirmed only after selected right-side bars close. SMC entries are market entries at the candle after the qualifying FVG closes (not limit fills at the FVG midpoint); stop is beyond the sweep wick plus configured buffer; target is fixed R. Signals use completed candles. If stop and target are both touched in one candle, the simulator assumes the stop was hit first (conservative). Position sizing is expressed in price units and risk percentage, not broker lots. Spread and commission are simplified; swaps, variable spread, slippage, contract size, tick value, broker execution rules and gaps may differ materially. This is a research tool, not proof of future profitability.

Liquidity sweep

Price trades beyond a prior swing high or low, then reclaims the level. A sweep alone is not an entry signal.

Market structure shift

Look for meaningful displacement and a break of a relevant swing. Distinguish a close from a wick.

Order block

A price area associated with the last opposing candle before displacement. Context and invalidation matter.

Fair value gap

A three-candle imbalance area that may act as a reaction zone, not a guaranteed fill or reversal.
